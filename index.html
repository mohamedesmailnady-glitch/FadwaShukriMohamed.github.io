About

Twelve years across design disciplines, now focused on 3D & AR

Fadwa's practice began in branding and illustration in 2012 and has expanded steadily since — through photography, packaging, publication and advertising design, interactive and UI work — arriving most recently at 3D modeling and its use in augmented reality.

She is a Ph.D. student and Teaching Assistant in the Graphic Design Department at the Faculty of Fine Arts, Alexandria University, Egypt.
